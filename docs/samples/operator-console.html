Action gate (Responsible Gambling Governor)

HARD holds cannot be overridden by a human approver. Jurisdiction spec-basis, gaming-license evidence completeness, payout arithmetic and patron compliance flags are independently recomputed, never trusted from the advisor's proposal; a real wager acceptance or payout settlement is always a human gaming supervisor's call, at every rollout phase.

OpGate
:wager/intakephase-3 auto-commit when clean, no capital risk yet -- the ONLY auto-eligible op in this domain
:jurisdiction/assessALWAYS human approval · spec-basis independently checked against wagering.facts, never fabricated
:patron/screenALWAYS human approval when clean · an unresolved compliance flag is a HARD, un-overridable hold instead
:wager/acceptALWAYS human approval · real money at risk (actuation/accept-wager) · evidence-completeness + double-acceptance guard enforced, never auto at any phase
:payout/settleALWAYS human approval · real money changes hands (actuation/settle-payout) · payout independently recomputed (stake × odds) + double-settlement guard enforced, never auto at any phase
